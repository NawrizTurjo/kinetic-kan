
Course Project Guidelines & Instructions

1. Group Formation Rules

   Group Size: Each group must consist of exactly 5 students.

   Cross-Subsection Grouping: Allowed. Students from different subsections within the same section (e.g., A1 and A2) can form a group.

   Cross-Section Grouping: Strictly Prohibited. Students from different sections (e.g., Section A and Section B) cannot form a group together.
2. Base Paper Selection & Project Scope

Every group must select a published base paper (journal or conference paper). The base paper can belong to any domain.

To help guide your work, here are a few suggested directions you may consider for your project (these are recommendations to inspire your topic, not rigid constraints):

    Cross-Domain Application: Apply numerical methods, simulation models, or data fitting/optimization algorithms covered in or related to the course (e.g., Monte Carlo methods, root-finding, interpolation, systems of equations) to solve a problem in a different application field.

    Methodological Extension: Take an existing paper in numerical analysis or simulation & modeling and extend its work (e.g., improving computational efficiency, relaxing assumptions, adapting the algorithm to a novel formulation, or enhancing stability).

    Exploratory & Comparative Study: Conduct an in-depth exploratory project (e.g., benchmarking multiple numerical/simulation approaches against complex real-world datasets or theoretical modeling benchmarks).
