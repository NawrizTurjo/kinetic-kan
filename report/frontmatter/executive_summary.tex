\section*{\sffamily\color{accent}Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}

This report documents a Kolmogorov-Arnold Network Neural ODE (KAN-ODE)
\cite{koenig2024kanode,liu2024kan,chen2018neuralode} project in four stages:
a baseline recipe reproducing the base paper's Lotka-Volterra and SIR
benchmarks; a diagnosis-and-fix cycle that resolved two cross-domain training
failures (damped pendulum, SIR); five independent follow-on studies probing
solver order, differentiation method, basis-function design, and stiffness;
and a validation stage (epoch-budget sensitivity, paper-implementation audit)
whose infrastructure is complete but whose runs have not yet executed.

\begin{keypoint}[Headline results]
\begin{itemize}
\item The pendulum failure ($R^2=-1.318$) was fixed by extending the
  training window, reaching $R^2=+0.647$ --- a $20\times$ full-horizon MSE
  improvement. SIR's failure traced to a unit-scale mismatch between the
  network's initial field and the true dynamics.
\item Adjoint sensitivity is strictly worse than direct autograd at this project's
  scale: it already uses less memory ($17.1$ vs.\ $18.6$--$21.5$~MB) but costs
  $1.9$--$2.1\times$ wall-clock, and no crossover point exists inside four orders
  of magnitude of trajectory length.
\item Gradient-norm roughness fails to explain Euler's convergence floor
  (Spearman $\rho=-0.088$, $p=0.87$); a shared, order-independent spike-burst
  structure dominates all six solvers' gradient traces.
\item A learnable spline/RBF basis blend never beats the better pure basis on
  either system tested, and on the pendulum it overfits catastrophically past
  $\sim$3{,}000 epochs in a way pure RBF does not.
\item The stiffness--solver stability analysis traced failures at
  $\mu\in\{0.5,1.0,2.0\}$ to random seed rather than solver order or numerical
  stiffness; the $\mu=2.0$ trap is a genuine, solver-independent spurious
  equilibrium in the learned vector field, confirmed directly from trained weights
  and from an independent weight-space loss-landscape analysis.
\item SINDy is far more noise-robust than KAN-ODE ($9\times$ vs.\ $1540\times$
  degradation, $\sigma=0\to0.1$) but strictly less accurate at every noise level
  tested; a real, non-mechanistic epidemic curve confirmed that two of SIR's
  three stability fixes transfer by the same mechanism that fixed SIR itself.
\end{itemize}
\end{keypoint}

Every number and figure in this report is traceable to a saved training
record or a regenerable figure from this project's own experiment logs.
